# Hotspot Topic Selection Quality Benchmark

Use this benchmark when generating or reviewing `topics` skill output.

## What Good Output Looks Like

Strong PR topic output reads like an editorial meeting note that can be executed immediately. It should show:

- What external information triggered the topic.
- Why the topic is timely now.
- Why the public or target users would care.
- Why this brand has a natural role.
- What material, interview, data, or first post can make the topic real.
- What should be discarded because it is evergreen, generic, risky, or too far from the brand.

## Evidence Anchors

Every candidate topic must include at least one anchor:

- Concrete date or near-term time window.
- Festival, solar term, season, school term, shopping season, agriculture/production cycle, or public-life scene.
- Policy window, regulatory action, industry meeting, report release, or official notice.
- Platform trend, hot list item, widely discussed post, creator behavior, or content format shift.
- Hot event, controversy, social debate, media report, competitor move, or benchmark case.
- Brand-owned proof such as a user case, product test, field experiment, report, dataset, or event, clearly marked as brand-owned rather than public news.

If the anchor cannot be verified from live data, mark it as `待补源` or `行业参照` and state the source type to verify.

## Required Topic Shape

Each final TOP5 item should include:

1. Topic title.
2. Information source basis.
3. Publication window.
4. Why now.
5. Public-interest or target-user concern.
6. Natural brand connection.
7. First content action.
8. Platform/channel.
9. Materials/interview/data leads.
10. Risk boundary.

## Anti-Patterns

Reject or downgrade topics that:

- Could be published any week with no loss of meaning.
- Only restate the brand's product message.
- Use broad labels such as AI empowerment, brand upgrade, industry trend, trust building, or social value without a concrete event or scene.
- Borrow a high-heat public event without a natural brand role.
- Lack verifiable source, material, interview line, or first content action.
- Use public welfare, disasters, social conflict, or vulnerable groups as decorative brand positioning.
